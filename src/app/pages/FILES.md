# Page Folders

Routed Angular screens.

## Folders

- `dashboard/` - Patient assessment dashboard and consultation history.
- `registration/` - Patient registration screen.
- `intake/` - Clinical symptom intake screen.
- `mri-upload/` - MRI upload screen.
- `rapport/` - Report viewing/export screen.
- `validation-queue/` - Specialist queue for reports awaiting validation.
- `case-evaluation/` - Specialist review screen for validating or rejecting a report.
